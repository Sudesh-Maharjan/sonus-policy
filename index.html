Sonus

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Sonus handles your information when you use the app to scan local audio, play music, search online tracks, and use playback features.

Last updated: April 15, 2026 No account required Local-first music player

1. Overview

Sonus is designed as a local music player. Most of your music data stays on your device. The app does not require you to create an account to use core features.

This privacy policy is provided for transparency and Play Store review.

2. Information We Collect or Access

Local audio files

Sonus requests access to audio files stored on your device so it can scan and display your music library. The app reads track metadata such as title, artist, album, duration, and file location so it can organize and play songs.

App preferences and history

Sonus stores favorites, recent searches, playback preferences, queue state, and other library information locally in SQLite on your device.

Voice search input

If you use voice search, Sonus requests microphone permission and processes your spoken query so it can search for songs. Voice processing is used only to provide the feature.

Online search terms

When you use online search, Sonus sends the text you type to the Jamendo API to fetch online tracks. Those search terms may be transmitted to the service provider you choose to use in the app.

3. How We Use Information

  • To scan, organize, and play your local audio library.
  • To store favorites, recent searches, playlists, and playback settings.
  • To display metadata and playback controls on the notification tray and lock screen.
  • To handle background playback and remote media controls such as play, pause, next, previous, and seek.
  • To send your online search queries to Jamendo when you explicitly use online discovery.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

  • Local storage: Library metadata and settings are saved on your device in SQLite. Depending on your permission settings, downloaded tracks may be stored in shared device media storage or app storage.
  • No account: Sonus does not require sign-in and does not sync your library to a cloud account.
  • Third-party services: Online search uses Jamendo. Their own privacy policy and terms may apply when you use that feature.
  • No selling: Sonus does not sell personal data.

5. Permissions

Sonus may request the following permissions depending on the features you use:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / audio library access: to scan and display your local songs.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show playback notifications and media controls.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: to keep music playing in the background.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: to enable voice search.
  • Media library permission: only if you choose to save downloads into shared device media storage.

6. Retention and Deletion

Most data Sonus stores is kept locally on your device. You can remove downloaded tracks, delete app data, or uninstall the app to clear local data. If a download is saved to shared device storage, it may remain on the device after uninstall until you delete it manually.

7. Children’s Privacy

Sonus is a general music player and is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children under the age required by local law without appropriate consent.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep local data on your device and minimize collection. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the app developer at sudechii.m@gmail.com.

11. Data Deletion Request

Sonus is an offline-first music player. We do not use user accounts, and most data is stored locally on your device.

  • Local Data: To delete your local data, including playlists, favorites, and settings, uninstall the app or clear the app data in your Android system settings.
  • Requesting Deletion: If you wish to request the deletion of any data that might have been processed, such as search queries sent to Jamendo or advertising identifiers, please contact us at sudechii.m@gmail.com.
  • Retention: We do not store your personal data on any servers. Advertising IDs are managed by Google and can be reset in your device's Google settings.
